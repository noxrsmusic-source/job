Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 January 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Nexoral Digital Pvt. Ltd. ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores and protects information when you use this website or engage our services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly through our forms, including:

  • Contact information — name, email address, phone number and company name.
  • Project information — service requirements, descriptions, budgets, timelines and reference links.
  • Application information — employment history, skills, portfolio links and documents you upload.
  • Files and documents — resumes, briefs, brand assets or reference material you choose to upload.
  • Technical information — browser type, device type and general usage data collected through Firebase services.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to enquiries, service requests and job applications.
  • To prepare proposals, quotes and project scopes.
  • To deliver contracted services and communicate about project progress.
  • To maintain internal records of client engagements and recruitment activity.
  • To protect our forms from spam and automated abuse using Firebase App Check.
  • To comply with legal and accounting obligations.

3. Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal data on the basis of your consent (when you submit a form), the performance of a contract (when you engage our services), and our legitimate interest in operating and protecting our business.

4. Data Storage and Security

Data submitted through this website is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Storage). We use:

  • Firebase Authentication with role-based administrator access.
  • Firestore and Storage security rules that prevent public read or write access to client and applicant data.
  • Firebase App Check to verify that requests originate from genuine browser sessions.
  • Encrypted transport (HTTPS/TLS) for all data in transit.

Only authorised administrators can access submitted applications and service requests. Applicants cannot view other applicants' data, and clients cannot view other clients' requests.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share limited information with:

  • Service providers who host our infrastructure (Google Firebase).
  • Professional advisers where required for legal or accounting purposes.
  • Authorities where we are legally required to disclose information.

6. Data Retention

We retain enquiry and application data for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was submitted, and in line with our legal and accounting obligations. Recruitment data is typically retained for up to 24 months unless you request earlier deletion.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Withdraw consent for processing where consent is the legal basis.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at the address shown in the footer.

8. Cookies and Local Storage

We use a minimal set of browser storage technologies:

  • Firebase Authentication tokens — required to keep administrators signed in to the admin panel.
  • Theme preference — a single localStorage entry remembering whether you chose light or dark mode.
  • Anti-spam timing data — a short-lived localStorage entry used to detect automated form submissions.

We do not use third-party advertising cookies on this website. Where an external sponsored link is presented, it is clearly labelled and opens in a new tab; clicking it is not required to submit any form.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are directed at businesses and professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16. If you believe a child has submitted data, contact us and we will remove it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. The "Last updated" date at the top will always reflect the current version.

11. Contact

For any privacy-related question or request, email us at the address listed in the site footer, or use the contact form.
